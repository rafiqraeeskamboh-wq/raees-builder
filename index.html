Why Raees Builder

Built properly the first time

Precast only saves you money when it is engineered, cured and installed correctly. That is the part we take seriously.

1

Controlled factory casting

Slabs are cast and cured in a controlled yard, not in the open, so strength is consistent from the first panel to the last.

2

Written estimate before work starts

You get a signed cost sheet covering material, transport and installation. The number you approve is the number you pay.

3

Faster completion, less mess

No shuttering, no long curing wait on site. Most roofs are placed and ready for finishing within a week of delivery.

4

One team, full responsibility

Manufacturing, transport and installation are all handled in-house, so there is a single point of accountability.

How It Works

Four simple steps

01

Call or WhatsApp
